# Licence

This repository has **no code**. It holds the release assets of the game's downloadable
packs (see [README.md](README.md)), and those packs are a collection of works by many
people under **different licences**. No single licence covers all of it: each part keeps
its own, listed with its author in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).

## What this means for you

- You may download the packs and use them **with the game**, which is what they are for.
- If you want to reuse a file in **another project, or commercially**, check its row in
  CREDITS.md first. The pickaxe and the intel allow no commercial use and no derivative
  works, and the models and sounds taken from GameBanana have no stated licence.
- If you redistribute the packs, keep CREDITS.md and this file with them.
- This is a statement of what the project knows about each file, not legal advice. If you are
  an author of something in these packs and want it credited differently or removed, please
  [open an issue](https://github.com/ngspades/assets/issues) and it will be done.
